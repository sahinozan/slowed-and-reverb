Slowed + Reverb for YouTube and Spotify

Slow down the song you're playing on YouTube, YouTube Music or Spotify and add reverb with one click. It changes the song while it plays, so there's nothing to download or convert.

Getting started

  1. Add it to Chrome or Firefox with one of the buttons above.
  2. Pin it so it's easy to find. Click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar. In Chrome, click the pin next to Slowed & Reverb. In Firefox, click the gear next to it and choose Pin to Toolbar.
  3. Play a song on YouTube, YouTube Music or Spotify in your browser, then click the Slowed & Reverb icon. Slowed + Reverb turns on right away.
  4. Change anything you like. The switch at the top turns it off again.

On Spotify, the first time you use it, the panel shows an Allow on Spotify button. Click it and accept your browser's question, and Spotify reloads once. Firefox asks the same way for YouTube.

Free to use

There's no paid version and no account to make. The extension doesn't collect anything about you or what you listen to, and it doesn't show ads. It only changes the sound inside your browser. The code is public on GitHub if you'd like to check.

Why I made it

I made this because the slowed + reverb extensions I tried were closed source and kept their best features behind a subscription. I wanted one I could trust and just use. So the code is public, it asks for as little access to your browser as it can, and every feature is free.
