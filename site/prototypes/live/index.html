Open, and yours.

The complete source is public under the MIT licence. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no paid tier. The extension never makes a network request of its own, and your presets stay in your browser.

It asks for access to a site the first time you open the popup there. Spotify stays untouched until you choose to allow it. Anything you allow can be taken back in your browser's extension settings.
