Slowed & Reverb

A browser extension. It slows the track, drops the pitch with it, and lets the sound ring out, live, on whatever is playing in the tab. Processed on your own computer.

Get for Chrome Get for Firefox

Side A

  1. Speed pitch follows, like tape0.5× to 1.5×
  2. Reverb a small room to a long tail0 to 100%
  3. EQ low, mid, high, a curve you drag±12 dB

Side B

  1. Keep original pitch slow down without droppingon or off
  2. Echo separate repeats0 to 100%
  3. Panleft to right
  4. Width mono to wider than your speakers0 to 200%
  5. Saturation a warm tape edge0 to 100%

A limiter is always on, so nothing distorts however far you push. Two presets are built in, Slowed + Reverb and Nightcore. Save your own, assign keyboard shortcuts, choose one of four looks.

The popup, actual size

Click the toolbar icon on the tab you are playing. Everything the extension does is in this panel.

Notes

Works on YouTube, YouTube Music and the Spotify web player, in desktop Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers, and in Firefox 142 or later. Other sites are left untouched. Live streams take every effect except speed.

Installing grants it nothing. It asks for access to a site the first time you open the popup there, and Spotify stays untouched until you allow it. There is no account, no analytics and no ads. Your music and your presets never leave your computer. The complete source is public under the MIT license.
