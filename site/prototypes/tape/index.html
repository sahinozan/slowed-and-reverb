Slow it down.

A browser extension that slows, softens and spreads out whatever is playing on YouTube, YouTube Music or Spotify, the way a tape deck with a lazy motor would. Drag the speed and watch the reels.

What it does

Speed runs from half to one and a half times, and the pitch drops or rises with it, exactly as tape does. Reverb runs from a small room to a long, washed-out tail. A three-band EQ sits underneath, drawn as a curve you drag.

An Advanced tab adds echo, pan, stereo width and a little saturation, plus a switch that keeps the original pitch while the track slows down. A limiter runs underneath so nothing distorts however far you push it.

What it does not do

It does not record, download or upload anything. Your music is processed on your own computer and your saved presets stay there too. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no paid tier. The complete source is public under the MIT license.

It asks for access to a site the first time you open the popup there, and leaves Spotify alone until you allow it. Anything you allow can be taken back in your browser's extension settings.

The whole thing fits in one panel.

Click the toolbar icon on the tab you are playing. Two presets come built in, Slowed + Reverb and Nightcore. Save your own under a name, assign keyboard shortcuts if you want them, and choose one of four looks.

Works on YouTube, YouTube Music and the Spotify web player, in desktop Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers, and in Firefox 142 or later. Live streams take every effect except speed.
