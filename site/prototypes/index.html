Three site prototypes

Each one is a standalone page. Nothing here is linked from the live site.

  1. Listen: the page is a player. Press play and drag the speed.
  2. Tape: a cassette whose reels slow down with the slider.
  3. Sleeve: the extension presented as a record release.
  4. Live: the real popup is the hero, wired to a loop; its palette recolours the page.
  5. Cassette: the tape deck in the extension's own colours, driving the same real popup.
